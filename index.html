How a baseline gets approved

The sequence I follow
STEP 1

Read the contract

Key dates, sections, programme specification, BOQ and the consultant's comment history.

STEP 2

Build the logic

WBS, activity codes, calendars and sequence from the construction method, not from a template.

STEP 3

Load it

Resources and cost tied to the BOQ, so progress and earned value come straight out of P6.

STEP 4

Check it

DCMA 14-point test, critical path review, open ends and constraints cleared.

STEP 5

Explain it

A narrative that answers the consultant's questions before they ask, then a clean comment log.
